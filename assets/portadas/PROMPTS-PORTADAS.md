# Prompts para portadas de Littera

Copia estos prompts en Bing AI Generator (https://www.bing.com/create).
Todos piden "no text" para anadir el titulo despues.

## 1. Grabado a la pluma

Japanese woodblock print style book cover, ukiyo-e aesthetic, a single wave and moon, muted colors, indigo blue and ochre accents, Hokusai-inspired, minimal composition, generous negative space, no text, vertical aspect ratio 2:3

## 4. Abstracto geometrico
Minimalist geometric abstract book cover, single bold circle in oxblood red on warm cream background, Bauhaus influence, perfect symmetry, generous negative space, flat vector style, subtle paper texture, no text, vertical aspect ratio 2:3

## 5. Fotografia en blanco y negro

## 6. Acuarela

## Modificadores

Estilo artistico:
- in the style of Gustave Dore
- in the style of Aubrey Beardsley
- in the style of Alphonse Mucha
- in the style of Hokusai
- in the style of Saul Bass

Ambiente:
- melancholic, wistful, contemplative
- joyful, warm, welcoming
- eerie, unsettling, dreamlike
- solemn, dignified, sacred

Paleta:
- cream paper and deep black ink
- terracotta, olive green and warm ochre
- indigo blue, oxblood red, cream
- sepia tones, aged photograph
- high contrast black and white

Composicion:
- generous negative space
- centered composition
- rule of thirds
- shot from above, flat lay
- dramatic low angle

## Como usarlas en Littera

1. Genera la portada en Bing.
2. Descargala a esta carpeta (assets/portadas/).
3. Anade el titulo y autor con GIMP, Photoshop o Canva.
4. Guarda como PNG a 1200x1800 px.
5. La Imprenta puede usar el archivo cuando integremos el sistema
   de portadas personalizadas.

## Proporciones utiles

- Portada de libro (2:3): 1200x1800, 1600x2400
- A5 libro pequeno: 1480x2100
- A4 (poster): 2480x3508
- Cuadrada (fanzine): 2000x2000
